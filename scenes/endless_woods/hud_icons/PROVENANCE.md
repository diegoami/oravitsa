# HUD icons

Hand-written SVG for Oravitsa (2026-10-29), dedicated to the public domain
(CC0). There are no third-party sources or generated art.

- `aura.svg`: a golden glowing halo. The HUD draws the aura number on top.
- `fed.svg`: a bowl with berries and a mushroom, beside the fed meter.
- `hungry.svg`: an empty, crossed-out bowl, shown while Oravitsa is starving.
