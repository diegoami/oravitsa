# Status icons

Hand-written SVG for Oravitsa (2026-10-28), dedicated to the public domain
(CC0). There are no third-party sources or generated art. Each icon is a 64 × 64 dark round badge, so it reads on any ground:

- `stunned.svg`: dizzy stars orbiting on a ring.
- `scared.svg`: sweat drops.
- `angry.svg`: anger mark (warning before a charge).
- `charging.svg`: impact burst.
- `fighting.svg`: scuffle cloud.
- `slowed.svg`: a snail, while the Slow skill holds a creature back (#59).
- `poisoned.svg`: a green drop with dark spots, over a pet a spider has bitten
  until Oravitsa cures it (#65).
- `wounded.svg`: a bandage with a red cross, over a pet a rat or goblin has
  bitten until Oravitsa cures it (#65).
