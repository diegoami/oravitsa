# Imported assets

Creator: **Quaternius**. These models are used under **CC0 1.0** as stated on
the individual pack pages, verified 25 October 2026.

- Animated deer, fox and wolf: [Ultimate Animated Animal Pack](https://quaternius.com/packs/ultimateanimatedanimals.html),
  [creator release](https://www.patreon.com/quaternius/posts/ultimate-animals-53427821).
  The creator-linked Drive download returned a quota-exceeded HTML page, which
  was not imported. The model was obtained from the public PixelTriks mirror.
- Birch and pine: [Ultimate Stylized Nature Pack](https://quaternius.com/packs/ultimatestylizednature.html),
  mirrored as part of its Ultimate Nature + Stylized Nature collection.
- License: <https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/>.

Original downloads are retained without geometry/animation edits. Runtime leaf
materials are duplicated and changed to alpha clipping with a muted lit
tint; animal size is normalized in code. No death/attack clips are invoked.
